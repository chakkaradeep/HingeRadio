On this band

    On the real phone

    The hinge angle would set the frequency directly, read through Apple's hinge API, with a haptic tick as a station locks. Closing the phone switches the radio off. Here the slider stands in for your hands.

    Where stations come from

    The dial fills from Radio Browser, a community-run open directory of internet radio. If it cannot be reached, a short built-in list is used. Your browser connects straight to each broadcaster, and a station that does not answer is crossed out.

    What is simulated

    The phone, the frequencies and the static. Internet stations have no real frequency, so each band spreads its stations along an FM-style scale.
